About Us

Motto: Raising Nation Builders

Vision: The vision of TribeArc Schools (including TribeArc Academy and International Secondary School) is centered on building a "new Nigeria" by transforming education to focus on technology, leadership, and business, aiming to create a generation of innovative, financially independent, and globally competitive "nation builders".

Mission: The core mission of TribeArc Schools is to unleash the potential of every student, empowering them to become the best version of themselves.
